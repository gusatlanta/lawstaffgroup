Common Questions

Fractional Counsel in Charlotte

How do I hire a fractional general counsel in Charlotte?
Tell LawStaff Group what you need (practice area, hours per week and jurisdiction) and we send you matched, video-interviewed counsel with their rates. You retain the lawyer you choose, and we handle billing and payroll.
Do fractional lawyers in Charlotte need to be licensed in North Carolina?
For advice on North Carolina law or appearances in North Carolina courts, generally yes. Many states also let lawyers admitted elsewhere register as in-house counsel for a single employer, and the rules vary. We match you with counsel admitted where your work requires.
Can I hire a part-time paralegal, contract administrator or legal assistant in Charlotte?
Yes. We place video-interviewed paralegals, contract administrators and legal assistants part-time or for a project. They work under the direction of your attorneys or legal team.
How much does fractional counsel cost in Charlotte?
Each professional sets their own hourly rate, based on experience and practice area. You pay one all-in rate (their rate plus our platform fee) on one invoice, and we quote it for everyone we present. Our Legal Salary Guide shows what legal professionals are asking to be paid.
Do I have to be in Charlotte to work with fractional counsel?
No. Most fractional engagements are remote-first, with on-site time when the work calls for it.
