Common Questions

Joining the Platform

Do I need to be an attorney?
No. We place attorneys and counsel, and also paralegals, contract administrators and legal assistants on fractional and part-time engagements. The application adapts to your role.
Does it cost anything to join?
No. Applying is free and there are no membership dues. LawStaff Group earns a set percentage of each engagement you're retained for, billed to the client as part of one all-in rate.
Who sets my rate?
You do. You'll tell us your target hourly rate in the application, and you can adjust it as you take on engagements.
How do I get paid?
Through LawStaff Group's payroll, by direct deposit on a regular schedule. We invoice the client for your hours, so you never chase a payment.
What's the video interview?
After you apply, a LawStaff recruiter reaches out to set it up. You answer a few questions on camera from your phone or laptop. Clients watch it before the first call, so the conversations you have are with people who already want to work with you.
Can I keep my own practice or other work?
Yes. Many fractional professionals combine engagements with their own practice or other commitments. You choose your hours, and attorneys clear conflicts before every engagement.
Does LawStaff Group direct an attorney's legal work?
No. We handle staffing, billing and payroll. An attorney's legal work and professional judgment are always their own.
