Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What is fractional counsel?
An experienced lawyer who works for your business part-time, for a set number of hours each week or month, instead of a full-time hire or an outside firm billing by the matter. A fractional general counsel fills the GC seat on a part-time basis.
How is talent vetted?
Every professional completes an application covering practice areas, experience and availability, then records a video interview. Attorneys also tell us their bar admissions and professional standing. We confirm bar admissions and check references before presenting anyone to a client.
Can I hire a part-time paralegal, contract administrator or legal assistant?
Yes. The same model applies: video-interviewed professionals, the hours you need, one invoice, and payroll handled by us. See legal support →
How does billing work?
Each professional sets their own rate. You receive one invoice from LawStaff Group for their hours plus our platform fee, a set percentage of each engagement. We run payroll and pay them directly, and we handle employer taxes and insurance.
Who directs the legal work?
You and your counsel. LawStaff Group handles staffing, billing and payroll. We never direct counsel's legal work or professional judgment.
Do you work with businesses outside the United States?
Fractional engagements are usually remote, so location matters less than licensing. We match businesses with counsel admitted in the jurisdictions their work requires.
I'm an attorney, paralegal or legal professional. How do I join?
Apply online in a few minutes, then record your video interview. Once you're approved, we present you to clients that fit your skills, rate and availability. Learn how joining works →
Do you still offer legal staffing?
Yes. We place attorneys, paralegals, legal secretaries and legal support professionals on a temporary, temp-to-hire and direct-hire basis, and we offer payroll services, as we have since 2003. See our hiring solutions →
