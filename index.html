Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What types of legal professionals does LawStaff Group place?
We place attorneys, paralegals, legal secretaries, legal administrators, litigation support professionals, legal office services staff, legal nurse consultants and legal IT professionals.
Do you offer temporary and temp-to-hire staffing?
Yes. We place legal professionals on a temporary, temp-to-hire and direct-hire basis. While a temporary employee is on assignment, we handle payroll, workers' compensation, employer taxes and unemployment insurance.
How do you screen candidates?
Our recruiters interview every candidate, qualify their skills, test their abilities with exams and check references to verify previous work history and performance. You are only presented with pre-screened, qualified candidates.
Why are the candidate profiles on this site anonymous?
Profiles show a candidate's title, location, experience and skills — but no name, employer or contact information. That protects candidate confidentiality. When you ask about a candidate, we make the introduction.
What are Payroll Services?
If you have already found a candidate but cannot add them to your own payroll right now — for example during a hiring freeze or a head-count restriction — we payroll your candidate for you and provide workers' compensation, unemployment and general liability coverage.
