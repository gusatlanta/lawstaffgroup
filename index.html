Why Fractional and Contract

Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What is fractional counsel?
An experienced lawyer who works for your business part-time, for a set number of hours each week or month, instead of a full-time hire or an outside firm billing by the matter. A fractional general counsel fills the GC seat on a part-time basis.
How do you screen candidates?
A LawStaff recruiter interviews every candidate, tests their skills and checks references, and we confirm bar admissions for attorneys. For each search we also build a screening questionnaire with your input, and every candidate we send answers it and records a video interview.
Can I hire a part-time paralegal, contract administrator or legal assistant?
Yes. We place legal support professionals part-time, on contract, temp-to-hire or permanently. Each one is video-interviewed, and on temporary work they're on our payroll. See legal support →
How does billing work?
On fractional, contract and temp-to-hire placements you pay one hourly rate for the hours you approve, covering the professional's pay and our fee. We quote it for every candidate before you interview, run payroll and handle employer taxes and insurance. For direct hire, we go over our placement terms before the search starts.
Who directs the legal work?
You and the attorney. LawStaff Group handles staffing and payroll. We never direct an attorney's legal work or professional judgment.
Do you work with businesses outside the United States?
Fractional and contract work is often remote, so location matters less than licensing. We match businesses with attorneys admitted in the jurisdictions their work requires.
I'm an attorney, paralegal or legal professional. How do I apply?
Apply online in a few minutes, then record your video interview right away or later from the link we email you. A LawStaff recruiter then starts matching you with roles that fit your practice, schedule and pay. See what happens after you apply →
Do you place permanent, full-time hires?
Yes. Alongside fractional and contract work, we place attorneys, paralegals, legal secretaries and legal support professionals on a temp-to-hire and direct-hire basis, and we offer payroll services, as we have since 2003. See our hiring solutions →
