What that means for a team of 10

At professional services employers, a 10-person legal team sees 5 or 6 departures a year. At the average private employer it's about 4. Either way, most of those openings are predictable.

A year is 12 times the average monthly rate. It counts departures, so one seat can turn over more than once.

What to do

Five ways to hire better this year

  1. Price to the ask. Put the middle-half range for the role in your job post. Candidates' floor is close to their ask, so low offers lose people.
  2. Sell the path, not just the pay. 22% are looking for growth. Show the next title, who they'll learn from, and what they'll own.
  3. You can ask for the office. 76% would work fully in the office and 74% would work hybrid.
  4. Screen for your system. Ask about your practice-management and e-filing systems up front. Train the rest.
  5. Build the bench before you need it. Turnover is predictable. Keep a short list of ready people behind every seat so the next resignation is a phone call, not a crisis.
