Legal Staffing for Midtown Firms and Legal Teams

Midtown is home to the Atlanta offices of many of the region's largest law firms, plus corporate legal departments and legal tech companies around Tech Square. Work here comes in surges: a document review with a production deadline, a trial team that needs two more paralegals for six weeks, or a deal room that needs contract attorneys to run diligence checklists.

That surge pattern is what contract legal staffing is built for. We place contract attorneys admitted in Georgia for review and diligence projects, litigation paralegals who know Relativity or similar review platforms, and docketing and records staff who can keep a large firm's calendar clean. The Northern District of Georgia courthouse and Fulton County courts are a short MARTA ride south, which helps when trial teams need people on site.

What We Screen For Here

  • Large-firm teams often need bodies fast and for a fixed project window
  • Review platform experience (Relativity, Everlaw, DISCO and similar) is screened up front
  • Conflict checks for contract attorneys are coordinated before anyone starts
