Forsyth County

Legal Staffing for Cumming Firms and Legal Teams

Cumming is the Forsyth County seat, and the county's rapid growth has pulled law firms north with it. Courthouse practice in Cumming covers family law, criminal defense, probate and civil disputes, while new subdivisions keep residential closing attorneys and estate planners busy.

Local firms tell us the hard part is finding experienced people who live nearby, because most legal talent in the region still commutes south on GA-400. We look for paralegals in Forsyth, Dawson, north Fulton and Hall counties first, and we place part-time help for solo practitioners around the Square who need steady weekly hours rather than a full-time hire.
