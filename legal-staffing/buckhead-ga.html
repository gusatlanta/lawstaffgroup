Legal Staffing for Buckhead Firms and Legal Teams

Buckhead has a deep bench of boutique law firms. Family law, trusts and estates, private-client tax, commercial real estate and plaintiff litigation practices fill the towers along Peachtree Road and Piedmont Road, and many of them run lean: one or two partners, a senior paralegal and an assistant who keeps everything moving.

That makes every support seat important. When a long-time legal secretary retires or a paralegal goes on leave in the middle of a custody trial or an estate administration, the firm cannot wait months. We place legal assistants who can handle a partner's calendar, client calls and probate or domestic-relations filings, and paralegals who have worked a file from intake through mediation. Buckhead and Lenox MARTA stations put much of the district within reach of candidates who do not drive in.

Common Questions

Legal Staffing in Buckhead

Can you cover our legal secretary during a medical leave?
Yes. Leave coverage is a common Buckhead request. A contract legal assistant works on our payroll for the length of the leave, and you approve hours weekly.
Do you place paralegals with family law experience in Buckhead?
Yes. Tell us the case mix, such as divorce, custody or support modification, and we screen for paralegals who have handled that work in Fulton or neighboring counties.
We are a two-attorney firm. Is that too small?
No. Many of our Buckhead placements are with small firms that need one strong support person, full time or part time.
Can a placement start as contract and become Direct Hire?
Yes. Temp-to-hire lets you see the work first, and you can make an offer when you are ready.
