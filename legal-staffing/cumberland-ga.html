Legal Staffing for Cumberland Firms and Legal Teams

Cumberland sits at the I-75 and I-285 interchange, and its office parks hold corporate headquarters, insurance claims operations and the defense firms that handle their files. Insurance defense, workers' compensation defense and construction litigation all have a strong presence here.

Defense work runs on volume and deadlines: medical record summaries, written discovery, deposition digests and reports to carriers. We place paralegals who have worked defense caseloads, including workers' compensation files before the State Board, plus corporate legal assistants and contract administrators for headquarters legal teams near the Galleria and Truist Park.
