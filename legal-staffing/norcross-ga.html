Legal Staffing for Norcross Firms and Legal Teams

Norcross and the neighborhoods along Jimmy Carter Boulevard and Buford Highway are among the most diverse in Georgia. Personal injury, immigration, workers' compensation and criminal defense firms serve a large Spanish-speaking client base here, and their busiest seats are intake and case management.

Plaintiff firms need case managers who can sign up a client, order medical records, track treatment and build a demand package, often in two languages. Immigration practices need paralegals who can assemble petitions and prepare clients for hearings at the Atlanta Immigration Court. We place both, plus legal receptionists who can handle a high-volume phone line in English and Spanish.
