Gwinnett County

Legal Staffing for Duluth Firms and Legal Teams

Duluth is home to a large Korean American business community, much of it along Pleasant Hill Road, and to the U.S. offices of several international companies. Law firms here handle immigration, business formation, commercial leases, employment and personal injury for clients who often prefer to work in Korean, Spanish or Vietnamese.

That makes language skill a real hiring requirement, not a bonus. We place bilingual legal assistants and paralegals and confirm their language ability before you interview. Corporate legal teams at U.S. subsidiaries also need contract administrators and compliance paralegals who can work across time zones with a headquarters overseas. Most Gwinnett County court filings go to the Justice and Administration Center in Lawrenceville, so local litigation experience there is a plus.
