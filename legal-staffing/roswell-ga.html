Legal Staffing for Roswell Firms and Legal Teams

Roswell's law practices tend to be neighborhood firms: residential real estate closing attorneys, estate planning and elder law offices, and family law practices clustered near Canton Street, Holcomb Bridge Road and Mansell Road. Many have been serving the same families for years.

Georgia residential closings are conducted by attorneys, so closing firms here need paralegals and processors who can open title orders, prepare settlement statements, chase payoffs and keep lenders on schedule. Estate planning firms need assistants who can prepare signing packets and manage trust funding follow-up. Spring and summer closing seasons push demand up, which is a good fit for contract and temp-to-hire placements.

What We Screen For Here

  • Closing volume is seasonal, so contract coverage often makes sense
  • Closing software experience (SoftPro, Qualia, ResWare and similar) is screened up front
