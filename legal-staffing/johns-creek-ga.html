Legal Staffing for Johns Creek Firms and Legal Teams

Johns Creek mixes two kinds of legal employers. Technology Park and the office corridor along Medlock Bridge Road (GA-141) hold technology, life sciences and manufacturing companies that file patents and trademarks and sign a steady flow of supply and licensing agreements. Around them, residential closing, estate and family law firms serve a suburb that has grown quickly.

For the companies, we place IP paralegals who can manage docket deadlines with outside counsel, trademark watch and renewals, and contract administrators for supply and licensing work. For the neighborhood firms, closing processors and legal assistants are the most frequent asks. Johns Creek has no MARTA rail, so we screen for commute fit early, and many candidates come from Duluth, Suwanee and Alpharetta.
