Legal Staffing for Marietta Firms and Legal Teams

Marietta is the Cobb County seat, and its legal market grew up around the courthouses near the Square. Superior, State, Probate and Magistrate Court practice keeps a dense ring of small and mid-size firms busy within walking distance of the courts: family law, criminal defense, personal injury, probate and general civil litigation.

Those firms hire for people who know local practice. A Cobb litigation paralegal who has prepared hundreds of hearing notebooks and knows how the clerk's office works is worth a lot to a five-lawyer firm. We place litigation and family law paralegals, legal assistants who can run a busy front desk and intake line, and part-time help for solo practitioners who need a few days of support each week.

What We Screen For Here
