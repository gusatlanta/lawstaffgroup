Athens-Clarke County

Legal Staffing for Athens Firms and Legal Teams

Athens has its own legal market, about seventy miles from Atlanta. Firms here serve northeast Georgia in the Western Judicial Circuit, which covers Athens-Clarke and Oconee counties, and handle personal injury, criminal defense, family law, real estate and university-related employment matters. The University of Georgia School of Law adds a steady supply of new lawyers and law clerks.

Distance cuts both ways. Athens firms cannot easily borrow help from Atlanta for on-site work, but plenty of paralegal and contract attorney tasks, such as discovery review, medical summaries and research memos, can be done remotely. We place local paralegals and legal assistants for courthouse-facing roles and Georgia-admitted contract attorneys who can work remotely on overflow projects.
