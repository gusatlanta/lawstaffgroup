Real Estate

What Fractional Real Estate Counsel Does

Fractional real estate counsel supports companies and investors with leases, acquisitions, development and financing, without a full-time real estate legal department.

Through LawStaff Group you retain experienced, video-interviewed counsel for the hours you need, from a few hours a month to several days a week. We run billing and payroll on every engagement, so your counsel just practices law.

Real estate paralegals handle closings, title review coordination and lease abstracts.

Typical Work

What They Handle

  • Commercial lease negotiation for tenants and landlords
  • Acquisitions and dispositions
  • Development and construction contracts
  • Real estate financing
  • Land use and zoning questions
  • Lease portfolio administration
  • Closing support with real estate paralegals

Common Questions

Fractional Real Estate Counsel

Can fractional counsel handle a multi-site lease portfolio?
Yes. Many companies use fractional real estate counsel to standardize lease positions and manage renewals across locations, often with a paralegal abstracting leases.
Do you provide real estate paralegals?
Yes. We place real estate and closing paralegals part-time, for a project or alongside fractional counsel.
Do you work on construction contracts?
Yes. Fractional counsel can negotiate construction and development agreements and coordinate with construction specialists for disputes.
