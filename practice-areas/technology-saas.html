Technology & SaaS

What Fractional Technology & SaaS Counsel Does

Fractional technology counsel handles the agreements and issues software businesses face every day: SaaS terms, enterprise negotiations, licensing, data, open source and AI.

LawStaff Group places experienced, video-interviewed attorneys for the hours the work needs, from a few hours a month to several days a week, or full time on contract. While they work with you on a fractional or contract basis, they're on our payroll.

Common Questions

Fractional Technology & SaaS Counsel

Can fractional counsel negotiate enterprise SaaS deals?
Yes. Enterprise negotiation is one of the most common reasons SaaS companies bring in fractional counsel, often with a fallback-position playbook for the sales team.
Do you help with AI terms and policies?
Yes. Counsel can draft AI product terms, review AI vendors and set internal responsible-use policies.
What about open source?
Fractional counsel can set an open-source policy, review license obligations and prepare for diligence questions about open-source use.
