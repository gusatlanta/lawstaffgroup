Workers' Compensation

What Fractional Workers' Compensation Counsel Does

LawStaff Group places workers' compensation attorneys and paralegals with claimant firms, defense firms and employers on a fractional, part-time or project basis: claim intake, medical records, hearings, mediations and settlements.

LawStaff Group places experienced, video-interviewed attorneys for the hours the work needs, from a few hours a month to several days a week, or full time on contract. While they work with you on a fractional or contract basis, they're on our payroll.

A part-time workers' comp paralegal can keep records, forms and board deadlines moving.

Typical Work

What They Handle

  • Claim intake and client communication
  • Medical records and treatment summaries
  • Hearing and mediation preparation
  • Settlement documents and approvals
  • Subrogation and lien coordination
  • Return-to-work coordination with employers
  • Docketing state board deadlines

How a LawStaff Search Runs

Common Questions

Fractional Workers' Compensation Counsel

Do you place workers' comp paralegals part-time?
Yes. We place video-interviewed workers' compensation paralegals for steady weekly hours or leave coverage, under your attorneys' supervision.
Can you match us with someone who knows our state's system?
Workers' compensation rules are state-specific, so we match you with attorneys admitted in your state and paralegals experienced with its forms and deadlines.
Do you work with both claimant and defense practices?
Yes. We place attorneys and paralegals on both sides, and you choose who fits your practice.
