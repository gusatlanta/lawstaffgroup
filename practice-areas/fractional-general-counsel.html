Fractional General Counsel

What Fractional Fractional General Counsel Counsel Does

A fractional general counsel is an experienced lawyer who serves as your company's GC on a part-time basis: setting legal priorities, handling day-to-day contracts and risk, advising leadership and the board, and managing outside counsel when you need specialists.

LawStaff Group places experienced, video-interviewed attorneys for the hours the work needs, from a few hours a month to several days a week, or full time on contract. While they work with you on a fractional or contract basis, they're on our payroll.

Pair your fractional GC with a part-time paralegal or contract administrator to keep volume moving.

Common Questions

Fractional Fractional General Counsel Counsel

What does a fractional general counsel do?
A fractional general counsel does what an in-house GC does (contracts, risk management, board and governance support, employment questions and managing outside counsel) for a set number of hours per week or month instead of full time.
When should a company hire a fractional GC instead of a full-time GC?
When legal work is steady but not a full-time job, or when you need senior judgment before you can justify a full-time salary. Many growing companies use a fractional GC from early growth through the point where in-house legal volume supports a full-time hire.
How many hours does a fractional general counsel work?
It depends on your needs: anywhere from a few hours a week to several days a week. You set the hours with your counsel and can adjust them as your workload changes.
