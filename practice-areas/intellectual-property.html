Intellectual Property

What Fractional Intellectual Property Counsel Does

Fractional IP counsel protects and commercializes what you've built (trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets and IP licensing) and coordinates patent specialists when you need them.

LawStaff Group places experienced, video-interviewed attorneys for the hours the work needs, from a few hours a month to several days a week, or full time on contract. While they work with you on a fractional or contract basis, they're on our payroll.

A paralegal can manage trademark docketing and renewal deadlines.

Typical Work

What They Handle

  • Copyright and content licensing
  • Trade secret protection and confidentiality programs
  • IP assignment and invention agreements
  • IP licensing in and out
  • Brand protection and enforcement letters

Common Questions

Fractional Intellectual Property Counsel

Can fractional counsel file trademarks?
Yes. Fractional IP counsel can run clearance searches, file applications and manage your trademark portfolio.
Do I need a patent attorney?
Patent prosecution before the USPTO requires a registered patent attorney or agent. We can match you with one, and your fractional counsel can coordinate.
How do we protect trade secrets?
With confidentiality agreements, IP assignment agreements, access controls and a clear policy, which fractional counsel can put in place quickly.
