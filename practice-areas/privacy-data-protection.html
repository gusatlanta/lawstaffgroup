Privacy & Data Protection

What Fractional Privacy & Data Protection Counsel Does

Fractional privacy counsel builds and runs a privacy program sized to your business: data mapping, policies, vendor terms and compliance with laws like the GDPR and U.S. state privacy laws.

LawStaff Group places experienced, video-interviewed attorneys for the hours the work needs, from a few hours a month to several days a week, or full time on contract. While they work with you on a fractional or contract basis, they're on our payroll.

A contract administrator can track data processing agreements and vendor reviews.

Typical Work

What They Handle

  • Privacy policies and notices
  • Data mapping and privacy program build-out
  • Data processing agreements and vendor terms
  • GDPR and U.S. state privacy law compliance
  • HIPAA questions for health data
  • Incident response planning and breach response
  • AI governance and responsible-use policies
  • Privacy reviews before fundraising or an acquisition

Common Questions

Fractional Privacy & Data Protection Counsel

Do we need a full-time privacy officer?
Not usually at first. Many companies use fractional privacy counsel to build the program and then keep a few hours a month for updates, vendor reviews and questions.
What's included in a privacy program build?
Typically a data map, updated privacy notices, internal policies, a vendor and data processing agreement process, an incident response plan and training, prioritized by your risk.
Can fractional counsel help with security questionnaires?
Yes. Counsel can answer the legal and privacy portions, prepare standard responses and work with your security team on the technical sections.
