What Fractional Startups & Venture Capital Counsel Does

Fractional startup counsel gives founders senior legal judgment from formation through Series A and beyond: entity setup, financings, equity, governance and the commercial contracts that come with growth.

Through LawStaff Group you retain experienced, video-interviewed counsel for the hours you need, from a few hours a month to several days a week. We run billing and payroll on every engagement, so your counsel just practices law.

Common Questions

Fractional Startups & Venture Capital Counsel

When does a startup need a general counsel?
Usually earlier than founders expect: around a first priced round, first hires or first enterprise customers. A fractional GC covers that stage without a full-time salary.
Can fractional counsel lead our financing?
Yes, for many seed and early-stage rounds. For larger or complex financings, fractional counsel often works alongside deal counsel and keeps costs in check.
Is fractional counsel cheaper than a law firm?
For ongoing work it is usually more predictable: you pay for set hours with one lawyer who knows your company, instead of matter-by-matter billing.
