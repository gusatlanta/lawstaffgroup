Fractional Counsel

Senior Legal Judgment, Without the Full-Time Hire

Most growing businesses need a great lawyer for some of the week, not all of it. A full-time general counsel is a big fixed cost, and outside firms bill by the matter, so the meter is always running.

Fractional counsel sits in between. You retain an experienced lawyer for a set number of hours, they learn your business, and you scale the hours up or down as the work changes.

LawStaff Group connects you with counsel who have already applied, recorded a video interview and had their bar admissions and references checked. Then we run the billing and payroll, so all you manage is the legal work.

Fractional General Counsel

A senior lawyer who acts as your general counsel on a part-time basis: commercial contracts, risk, employment issues, board matters and managing outside counsel.

Ideal for growing companies that need GC-level judgment before they're ready for a full-time GC, or that want to keep legal spend predictable.

Part-Time In-House Counsel

Standing weekly hours embedded with your team. Typical work includes contract review and negotiation, employment questions, privacy and compliance programs, and day-to-day legal support for sales and operations.

Already have a legal department? Add part-time counsel to absorb volume without adding headcount.

Project Counsel

Defined scope, defined timeline. Bring in counsel for a financing, an acquisition, a contract-template overhaul, a privacy program build-out or a vendor consolidation, and wrap up when the project's done.

Interim & Leave Coverage

Cover parental leave, a departure or the gap while you search for a permanent hire. Interim counsel step in quickly and keep deadlines and deals moving.

Fractional Legal Support

Paralegals, Contract Administrators and Legal Assistants

The same model, for the people who keep legal work moving: video-interviewed professionals, the hours you need, one invoice and payroll handled by us. They work under the direction of your attorneys or legal team.

Paralegals

Litigation, corporate, real estate, employment and compliance paralegals: trial preparation, e-filing, discovery, entity management, closings and due diligence.

Retain one for steady weekly hours, a single matter or a busy trial calendar.

Contract Administrators

Contract intake and triage, playbook-based first reviews, signature routing, repository clean-up, renewal and obligation tracking, and contract lifecycle (CLM) administration.

Ideal when sales and procurement are waiting on legal, or nobody owns the contract repository.

Legal Assistants

Calendaring and docketing, court filings, document management, billing entries, correspondence and the administrative work that keeps attorneys billing.

Add part-time support for a busy practice group or cover a leave without a new hire.

Common Questions

Hiring Fractional Talent

How is this different from hiring a law firm?
A law firm bills you by the matter, and you often work with a rotating team. Fractional counsel works for your business for a set number of hours, learns how you operate and becomes part of your team, the way an in-house lawyer would.
What does it cost?
Each professional sets their own rate. You pay one all-in rate: their rate plus our platform fee, a set percentage of each engagement. The fee covers screening, invoicing, payroll, employer taxes and insurance. We'll quote the all-in rate for everyone we present.
Can I change the hours later?
Yes. Fractional engagements are built to flex. Increase hours for a transaction or a busy quarter, and reduce them when things slow down.
Who directs the legal work?
You and your counsel. LawStaff Group handles staffing, billing and payroll. We never direct counsel's legal work or professional judgment.
Who supervises fractional paralegals, contract administrators and legal assistants?
Your attorneys or legal team, just as they would supervise an employee in the same role. Legal support professionals don't give legal advice; they work under the direction of the lawyers they support.
Can they join us full time later?
Talk to us. Many engagements start part-time and grow. We'll work with you on a transition that fits both sides.
