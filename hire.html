Attorneys

Attorneys for Law Firms and Legal Departments

Law firms bring us in when the caseload gets ahead of the team: a trial date, a stack of new files, an associate on leave. Companies bring us in when legal work is steady but not yet a full-time job, or when the legal department needs another pair of hands.

Either way, a LawStaff recruiter learns the work first. For every search we build a screening questionnaire with your input, and each candidate we send comes with their answers, a recorded video interview, checked references and confirmed bar admissions.

On fractional and contract work, the attorney is on our payroll. You direct the legal work; we handle pay, employer taxes and insurance.

Fractional General Counsel

An experienced attorney who handles your company's legal work for a set number of hours each week or month: commercial contracts, employment questions, risk, board matters and coordinating outside firms.

A fit for companies that have outgrown calling outside counsel matter by matter, but don't yet have the volume for a full-time general counsel.

Contract Attorneys for Law Firms

Attorneys admitted in your state to take depositions, cover hearings, draft motions and written discovery, or carry a block of files. Common in personal injury, insurance defense, workers' compensation and commercial litigation.

Billed hourly for the hours worked. LawStaff Group never takes a share of your legal fees.

Leave & Vacancy Coverage

When an attorney goes on leave or gives notice, a contract attorney can hold the seat, keep deadlines and closings on track, and hand the work back cleanly when the seat is filled.

In-House Hires, Temp-to-Hire or Direct

Adding an attorney to your legal department? Start them on our payroll and move them onto your staff once you're sure, or let us run a direct-hire search for a permanent seat, backed by our replacement guarantee.

Legal Support

Paralegals, Contract Administrators and Legal Assistants

Temp, temp-to-hire, fractional or permanent. Support professionals work under the direction of your attorneys or legal team, and on temporary assignments we call on the first day and every week after to make sure it's working.

Paralegals

Litigation, corporate, real estate, employment and compliance paralegals: trial preparation, e-filing, discovery, entity management, closings and due diligence.

Bring one in for steady weekly hours, a single matter or the weeks before trial.

Contract Administrators

Contract intake and triage, playbook-based first reviews, signature routing, repository clean-up, renewal and obligation tracking, and contract lifecycle (CLM) administration.

A good fit when contract volume has outgrown the people handling it.

Legal Assistants

Calendaring and docketing, court filings, document management, billing entries, correspondence and the administrative work that keeps attorneys billing.

Add part-time support for a busy practice group or cover a leave without a new hire.

Legal Secretaries & Office Staff

Legal secretaries, legal administrators, litigation support, receptionists and file clerks, plus legal nurse consultants for medical-heavy caseloads.

Common Questions

Hiring Through LawStaff

Which arrangement is right for us?
If the work is ongoing but part time, go fractional. If it has an end date, such as a trial, a leave or a project, go contract. If you want to see someone's work before you make an offer, go temp-to-hire. If you already know it's a permanent seat, go direct hire. Not sure? Call us and we'll talk it through.
What does it cost?
Fractional, contract and temp-to-hire professionals are billed at one hourly rate for the hours you approve. It covers their pay and our fee, and we quote it for every candidate before you interview. For a direct-hire search, we go over our placement terms before we start.
Can we change the hours later?
Yes. Add hours for a trial, a closing or a busy quarter, and scale back when things are quiet.
Who directs the legal work?
You and the attorney. LawStaff Group handles staffing and payroll. We never direct an attorney's legal work or professional judgment.
Who supervises paralegals, contract administrators and legal assistants?
Your attorneys or legal team, just as they would supervise an employee in the same role. Legal support professionals don't give legal advice; they work under the direction of the lawyers they support.
Can we hire them permanently?
Yes. That's what temp-to-hire is for, and fractional and contract placements can convert too. When you're ready, we work together to move them onto your staff.
